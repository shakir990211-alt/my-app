Return & Refund Policy

Our Return Policy

  • Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached.
  • Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after we receive the item.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method (demo mode - no real charges).
  • Some items like groceries and personal care may not be returnable for hygiene reasons.

How to Return

  1. Go to My Orders.
  2. Select the order and item you want to return.
  3. Click Return or Replace Items and follow the steps.
  4. Drop off the package at the nearest pickup point.
